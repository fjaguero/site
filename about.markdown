I'm Head of Product and CISO at [Lang.ai](https://lang.ai), where we build AI agents that analyze customer data to drive retention and growth. Our solution helps businesses transform unstructured customer interactions into actionable priorities using custom AI models. You can learn more about my work experience in my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fjaguero/).

## Side Projects

In 2017, I published [The Success Hacker](https://thesuccesshacker.es/), a book about productivity. In 2019, I started [In Progress Show](http://inprogressshow.com), a podcast exploring how tech companies work from the inside.

## Community Leadership

_Open Source & Web_

I've been an active contributor to [Mozilla Hispano](https://www.mozilla-hispano.org/documentacion/Usuario:Fjaguero) for over 12 years, helping promote openness and innovation on the Web. My contributions are [acknowledged](https://www.mozilla.org/credits/) in Mozilla's official credits. Throughout my involvement, I've shared insights about open source and web technologies through talks in Argentina, Spain, Peru, and Poland.

_Developer Communities_

Founded [ValenciaJS](https://medium.com/the-web-is-the-platform/introducing-valenciajs-1a73d39a9c94) (2015) to empower the JavaScript community in Spain's third-largest city, and launched [MadridDevs](https://medium.com/@fjaguero/introducing-madrid-devs-2712c6c38e3c), a public Slack workspace for local developers.

_Language Learning_

Served as Duolingo Global Ambassador (2017-2020), organizing bilingual [events in Madrid](http://events.duolingo.com/madrid/) and helping establish one of the most active language exchange communities in Europe.
